About the club

The Elm Street Garden Club began in 1974 when six families asked the council for the field behind the school. Today we have forty members and two acres.

Membership costs twenty pounds a year. It pays for seeds, water and the shed roof.

Who does what

Ruth chairs the meetings. Tom keeps the books. Everyone else turns up with a spade.

Elm Street Garden Club, founded 1974. Meetings every second Tuesday at the community hall.
